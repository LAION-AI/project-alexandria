# Unpublished original report

Status: **historical, unreleased, and not peer reviewed**. Source: `alexandria_blog.pdf` supplied
with the experiment workspace. The report predates the terminology shift from “knowledge graph”
to “Knowledge Unit” and contains exploratory results that were omitted or revised in arXiv v2.
Numbers are transcribed to preserve the experiment record; they are not presented as new reruns.

## Abstract-level MCQ experiment

The report describes 1,000 abstracts in each of computer science, life sciences, physics, and
mathematics; five generated MCQs per abstract; and four conditions: no context, original text,
generated graph, and text reconstructed from that graph.

## Chunk-size ablation

For medical full papers evaluated with Llama 3.1 8B, the report found better KU-context results
with 200-word chunks than with 500-word chunks.

| Context | 500 words | 200 words |
|---|---:|---:|
| No context | 60.01 | 58.58 |
| Original | 90.26 | 90.46 |
| KG | 72.87 | 75.03 |
| Reconstructed | 72.46 | 77.45 |

## Planted-error detection

Two hundred graphs (physics and life sciences) received one to six serious errors generated by
Llama 3.1 70B. Detection success rates were:

| Domain | Evaluator | Mean success | SD |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Physics | Llama 3.1 8B | 79.04 | 30.95 |
| Physics | Llama 3.1 70B | 78.18 | 36.40 |
| Life sciences | Llama 3.1 8B | 86.78 | 22.54 |
| Life sciences | Llama 3.1 70B | 94.98 | 14.83 |

The report explicitly says no statistical-significance test was run.

## Self-correction

Self-correction comprised draft generation, an error report, and a corrected graph. With Mixtral
8x7B as the answering model, KG accuracy rose from 79.23 to 82.08 in medical and 70.03 to 72.21
in physics. With Llama 3.1, it fell from 75.03 to 70.72 in medical and rose from 66.53 to 70.54
in physics. Including the error report directly as QA context was not beneficial.

| Answer model | Domain | Condition | No SC | With SC |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Mixtral 8x7B | Medical | KG | 79.23 | 82.08 |
| Mixtral 8x7B | Medical | Reconstructed | 77.14 | 80.97 |
| Mixtral 8x7B | Physics | KG | 70.03 | 72.21 |
| Mixtral 8x7B | Physics | Reconstructed | 69.39 | 73.00 |
| Llama 3.1 | Medical | KG | 75.03 | 70.72 |
| Llama 3.1 | Medical | Reconstructed | 77.45 | 70.14 |
| Llama 3.1 | Physics | KG | 66.53 | 70.54 |
| Llama 3.1 | Physics | Reconstructed | 70.16 | 70.29 |

## Previous-KU context summaries

The report prepended a three-sentence summary of the previous ten KUs. With Mistral Small 22B,
medical KG accuracy rose from 80.03 to 82.90, while physics changed from 74.09 to 74.20. The
physics reconstruction score fell from 80.13 to 75.67, so the ablation does not support a blanket
claim that summaries help every downstream representation.

| Domain | Condition | Without summary | With summary |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Medical | KG | 80.03 | 82.90 |
| Medical | Reconstructed | 83.95 | 84.65 |
| Physics | KG | 74.09 | 74.20 |
| Physics | Reconstructed | 80.13 | 75.67 |

With context summaries already present, self-correction increased KG accuracy from 82.90 to 84.24
in medical and from 74.20 to 75.82 in physics; reconstructed accuracy changed from 84.65 to 84.78
and from 75.67 to 77.29, respectively.
