Scientific-Summaries QA authoring protocol (executed by three gpt-6-luna agents)

Read the complete saved paper text assigned in your shard. Do not read its summary,
other authors' questions, judge responses, or benchmark scores. Generate exactly
ten English multiple-choice questions per paper. Each has four choices A–D and
one unambiguous correct answer. Questions should be difficult and require specific
knowledge from this paper, not general scientific trivia. Prefer quantitative
results, experimental/mathematical methods, interpretation, mechanisms, and caveats.
Use plausible, specific distractors; do not add obviously absurd options.

For every question provide:
- question and options {A, B, C, D}
- answer: A, B, C, or D
- rationale explaining why the gold answer follows from the paper
- evidence_quote: a contiguous exact passage containing the supporting facts
- evidence_start and evidence_end: null (filled by deterministic source alignment)
- difficulty: hard
- category: result, method, limitation, or mechanism

Save one JSON file per document:
{document_id, author_model: "gpt-6-luna", questions: [...]}
Use only the saved fulltext as evidence. Quotes may retain PDF wrapping or ligatures;
alignment allows whitespace and Unicode NFKC normalization, never fuzzy matching.
Evidence must include the relevant numbers/formulas/facts, not just a sentence intro.
Full sentences or paragraphs are acceptable. Validate all quotes with evidence_span
from evaluate.py; repair any failure before considering a document complete.

Root assigns balanced gold positions deterministically without changing option
content. Gold answers, evidence, and rationales are never included in judge prompts.
Judge evaluation proceeds while authors are still producing other papers' questions.
Question/option/gold changes invalidate that paper's checkpoint; evidence-only edits
do not change judge inputs and can be merged without repeating inference.
