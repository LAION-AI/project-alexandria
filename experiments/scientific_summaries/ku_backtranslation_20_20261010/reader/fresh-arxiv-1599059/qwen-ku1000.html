Recorded context summary

The paper proposes a scalable physical implementation of the adiabatic Grover search algorithm using atoms in a cavity. The system utilizes an ensemble of three-level atoms where a marked atom is distinguished by an energy shift. The process, termed inverse fractional STIRAP, achieves the search with a time scaling of the square root of the database size.
