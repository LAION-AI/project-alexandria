Recorded context summary

In the two-well regime, the tip apex delocalizes due to rapid thermal jumps, causing the cantilever to move in an effective potential defined by the mean tip position. The mean surface force is reduced and the effective potential is flattened due to the competition between increasing potential energy and entropy from apex disorder. Discontinuities in the mean force correspond to transitions between one-well and two-well potential shapes, occurring over a timescale determined by the jump rates.
