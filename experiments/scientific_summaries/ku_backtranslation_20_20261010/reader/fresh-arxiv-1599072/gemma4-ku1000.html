Recorded context summary

The study introduces a two-mass, two-spring model to explain the fine structures in the oscillating motion of atoms during friction force microscopy (FFM). The model demonstrates that the tip can be completely or partially decoupled due to an extremely low effective mass, a finding that is supported by agreement with current high-resolution experiments. The authors use a hybrid computational scheme to simulate the interplay between rapid tip motion and slow cantilever motion, showing that dissipation depends sensitively on surface corrugation.

The text details a two-mass, two-spring model for atomic friction, where the tip apex delocalizes due to ultra-low effective mass and high surface corrugation.
