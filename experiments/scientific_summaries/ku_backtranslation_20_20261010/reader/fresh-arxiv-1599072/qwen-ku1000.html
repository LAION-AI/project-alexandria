Recorded context summary

The study analyzes a two-mass-two-spring model to explain fine structure in Friction Force Microscopy (FFM) slip events. It demonstrates that the tip apex can be delocalized due to rapid thermally activated motion, which affects the measured lateral force. This model provides a theoretical explanation for recent experimental observations that contradicted the traditional Tomlinson model, which was based on a single spring.
