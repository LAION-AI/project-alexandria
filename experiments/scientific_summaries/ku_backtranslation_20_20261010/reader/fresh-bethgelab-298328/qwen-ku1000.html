Recorded context summary

The study investigated whether Musician's Dystonia involves distorted finger representations in the primary sensorimotor cortex using fMRI. Results indicated that somatotopic organization remained intact in musicians with dystonia, contradicting the hypothesis of spatial distortion. The findings suggest that task-specific dystonia stems from higher-order disruptions in skill encoding rather than primary sensory map abnormalities.
