Recorded context summary

The study investigated whether Musician's Dystonia involves maladaptive neural changes to finger representations in the primary sensorimotor cortex. Using fMRI on musicians with and without the condition, the researchers found that digit representations remain intact and similar between groups. These findings suggest that the disorder arises from higher-order skill encoding disruptions rather than distorted somatotopy.
