Recorded context summary

The study investigated finger representation in the primary sensorimotor cortex of musicians with dystonia using functional MRI. The research found that digit representations were intact in musicians with and without dystonia, contradicting hypotheses of somatotopic distortion. The authors suggest that task-specific dystonia results from higher-order skill encoding disruptions rather than primary cortical map changes.
