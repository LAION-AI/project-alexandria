Recorded context summary

The study investigated finger representation in the primary sensorimotor cortex of musicians with dystonia using functional MRI. It found that while spatial metrics showed clear somatotopy, representational similarity analysis indicated no difference in digit representation architecture between musicians with and without dystonia. The authors conclude that the neural representation of the generic finger cards is preserved, suggesting that the observed, specific dystonia is due to impairments in the encoding of higher cognitive abilities, rather than a distortion of hand-somatotopy.
