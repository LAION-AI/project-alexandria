What was improved

Terminology protection. Multiword definitions explicitly present in the current paper, together with a finite list of technical expressions such as “regions of interest”, “degrees of freedom”, “confidence interval”, “standard error” and “false discovery rate”, may no longer be split by insertions. This addresses an actual v4 meaning flag. Original formula, code and quotation spans retain their separate protection.

Definitions and text extraction. Unicode NFKC normalization handles PDF ligatures before definition matching and duplicate-gloss detection. Mixed-case, plural and hyphenated initialisms can align with source definitions. A mixed-case initialism still requires all its letters in order and a minimum of matching word initials; ambiguity blocks expansion. Additional fixed reference aliases require their canonical full term to occur in this same source, and explicit source conflicts take precedence. Source hashes, inventory and provenance remain saved.

Grammatical coverage. The methodological gerund vocabulary now includes examining, combining, selecting, clustering, normalizing and other concrete operations. Purpose constructions remain restricted to verbs expressing design/use/engineering. A fixed set of head nouns permits provenance relatives, such as “journals that are from …”. Singular numeric reference labels may become “Figure number 1”; the identifier is never split or respelled. Bare parallel passives and infinitives may repeat an already present auxiliary or “to”; intervening negation, objects, adverbs and hedges make those rules ineligible.

Measurements. Scientific notation and guarded numeric ranges remain exactly written as before. Unit glosses include angstroms, context-checked flux/current/energy/force/length symbols, spaced percent units and enumerated inverse units. Range annotations use plural unit names. Compact K/m/Å require explicit physical cues. Compound units remain whole. No value is converted or rescaled, and uncertain symbols remain ineligible.

Planner and preservation checks

Candidates are generated once on the original fragment. Connected copied intervals are searched jointly, admitting individually zero-gain intermediate steps. A committed plan must strictly reduce covered original words, covered total words, matching source six-grams and covered fraction. Each summary has a 3% added-word cap. Every original character and normalized word must remain in order; numerical, unit, Greek-variable, operator, acronym, modal, negation and epistemic signatures must agree. Evidence quotes and bibliographic metadata are untouched and audited separately.

All cohort source-copy audits ran on the compute allocation using the established whitespace-word normalization and a separate punctuation-split diagnostic. All 97 sources and original 970 gold/question slots are retained. Failures and rollbacks remain visible.
