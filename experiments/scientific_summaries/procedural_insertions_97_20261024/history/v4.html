What changed

The catalog has 33 families. This version adds bounded duration phrases without changing their bounds, fixed postnominal “of interest” relatives, and omitted object relative markers before an explicit passive clause. The reduced-relative noun vocabulary is expanded. No certainty, frequency, causality, exclusivity or uncertainty fillers are added.

Additional exact unit names include femtoseconds, picoseconds, terahertz, picograms, pressure and context-checked parts-per-million/billion notation. A finite list of compound units such as mg/mL, g/L and m/s is explained as a whole, with no conversion or rescaling. Molarity, speed and ambiguous single-letter symbols retain positive-context checks. Pure numeric percentage parentheses remain contiguous and receive their explanation outside the original parentheses; prose parentheses use square explanatory brackets. Existing adjacent explanations are not repeated.

Hyphenated initialisms such as ICP-OES can now align with their source definitions. Singular/plural variants are consolidated only for a fixed set of regular technical head nouns; genuinely conflicting definitions still prevent expansion. Each glossary entry records its source and provenance. The conservative arm omits acronym glosses and complex reduced relatives.

Selection and integrity

Candidates are generated once on each original fragment. The component-wise joint planner can combine individually zero-gain insertions, but commits only a plan that decreases original covered words, total covered words, source six-gram hits and covered fraction. A 3% whole-summary added-word limit applies. Every original character and normalized word must remain in order, original math/quote/code spans must remain contiguous, and numerical, unit, operator, Greek-variable, acronym, modal, negation and epistemic signatures must agree. Evidence quotes and metadata are left untouched and audited separately.

102 targeted checks passed before code hashes were frozen and the full job was submitted. Every final summary was checked against its own full untruncated source using the established whitespace-word metric and a separate punctuation-split diagnostic. All 97 source hashes and all original question/gold slots are retained. Rollbacks remain visible.

Meaning and QA

The pinned Qwen2.5-7B student uses the original prompts, parser, sampling and paired seeds on every original MCQ. Only exact-context predictions are reused; this run generated 400 new student answers. The same pinned 27B reviewer judges all changed sentence/window pairs, reusing only exact pairs. Labels are automatic and do not prove human equivalence; all non-equivalent joint-arm flags are shown below.

This 97-paper benchmark has been repeatedly reused for development. The small QA differences are exploratory and do not establish a generalization gain. Glosses and function words change literal strings while retaining the original wording; they are surface elaborations, not independently written conceptual paraphrases. Meaningful reuse and appropriate attribution still matter.
