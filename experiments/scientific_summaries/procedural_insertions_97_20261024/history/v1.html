The rule system

The catalog contains 30 families: complementizers and quantifiers; explicit purpose/instrument/process phrases; unambiguous physical quantity elaborations; temporal phrases; textual and past-tense reduced relatives; exact unit-name parentheses; and initialism definitions obtained from the same paper. The core arm excludes acronym glosses and both relative-clause families.

Unit names use a finite exact dictionary and never rescale a quantity. Single-letter symbols need context: h can be Planck’s constant and g can denote gravitational acceleration. Original formula/quote/code spans cannot be interrupted. A dimension qualifier cannot capture a coordinated quantity in a different dimension. Typed parenthetical unit explanations may resemble functions to a generic regex; they are explicitly recognized as explanatory language.

The source parser found 258 unambiguous two-to-five-word initialism definitions across the 97 sources. Initials must align exactly (with explicit handling of hyphens); ambiguous/multiple definitions and mathematical/numeric glosses are rejected. This is a finite heuristic, not a complete entity-linking model. Every paper’s definition inventory and source hash are saved.

How joint planning differs from the old search

The planner starts from the original full-source six-gram matches and groups connected copied-word intervals. It generates candidates on the original fragment, preventing repeated self-expansion. Within each component it keeps up to 24 candidate plans and explores up to six insertions. An intermediate state may leave copied-word coverage unchanged. Only the final plan must make a strict improvement. Overlapping explanations of the same quantity are incompatible; each summary has a 3% added-word budget.

The matched catalog in the greedy arm provides the search ablation. Its actual copied-word coverage is 5.1478%, versus 5.1444% for joint planning. Search statistics, not the illustrative fixture, establish how much this helps on the 97 real papers.

A targeted 20-word fixture verifies the former failure: neither legal unit-name insertion alone reduces covered original words, but both together break the middle segment. The greedy planner leaves the fixture unchanged; joint planning finds the two-insertion solution. Thirty-eight catalog/provenance/adversarial checks pass.

Meaning and QA evaluation

The unchanged Qwen2.5-7B student answers every original MCQ under fixed prompts, parser, sampling and paired seeds. Exact same contexts reuse saved predictions; this run needed 490 new student calls. A pinned 27B teacher compares all final original/edited sentence pairs with thinking disabled, using exact-pair caching. It judges scope, certainty, quantity, reference, causal meaning and grammar. Automatic labels are not human equivalence guarantees.

Numbers, unit quantities, Greek variables, operators, acronyms, modal words, negation and an explicit epistemic-word inventory must be preserved. Every original character and normalized word remains in order; original mathematical spans remain contiguous. Those checks do not prove general semantic equivalence. The 97-paper cohort has been reused repeatedly and this is exploratory; future generalization needs fresh papers.
