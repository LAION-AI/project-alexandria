Executive Summary
This paper investigates the Green-Schwarz (GS) sigma-model for the N=2 superstring in a D=2+2 target space-time, demonstrating that its action can be reformulated using Cayley's hyperdeterminant. The authors show that when the zweibein field is eliminated from the GS action, the resulting action contains the Nambu-Goto (NG) action, which is equivalent to the square root of Cayley's hyperdeterminant. This formulation exhibits manifest SL(2,R) x SL(2,R) x SL(2,R) symmetry. The target space-time D=2+2 is suitable for self-dual supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. The action also possesses fermionic κ-symmetry, which is a criterion for its light-cone equivalence to the Neveu-Schwarz-Ramond (NSR) formulation for the N=2 superstring. The primary finding is that the NG-action part of the GS sigma-model, after eliminating the 2D metric, can be expressed as the square root of Cayley's hyperdeterminant, revealing the manifest [SL(2, IR)]3 symmetry. This formulation is classically invariant under fermionic κ-symmetry. The work highlights the relationship between Cayley's hyperdeterminant, the N=2 superstring, and self-dual supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. A key limitation is that the quantum equivalence of the NG reformulation to the conventional NSR superstring is not claimed. The significance lies in providing a new perspective on the symmetries of the N=2 superstring and its connection to integrable systems.

Research Context
The study builds upon the recent discovery that the Nambu-Goto (NG) action with D=2+2 target space-time exhibits manifest global SL(2, IR) x SL(2, IR) x SL(2, IR) symmetry, where the square root of the determinant of pull-backs can be rewritten as Cayley's hyperdeterminant. The existing Neveu-Schwarz-Ramond (NSR) formulation has a drawback in rewriting it purely in terms of a determinant due to fermionic superpartners. The Green-Schwarz (GS) formulation, while offering advantages, faces an obstruction because its kinetic term is not of the NG-type. This work addresses the obstruction in the GS formulation by eliminating the zweibein field, which allows the action to be expressed in terms of the NG-type determinant form, thus revealing the manifest symmetry. Prior work includes the use of Cayley's hyperdeterminant in quantum information theory and the entropy of the STU black hole, but this paper focuses on its application to the N=2 superstring.
