ABSTRACT. Let p be a prime number. Mazur proved that a geometrically maximal unramiﬁed
abelian covering of X0(p) over Q is given by the Shimura covering X2(p) →X0(p), that is, a
unique subcovering of X1(p) →X0(p) of degree Np := (p −1)/ gcd(p −1, 12). In this short
paper, we show that a geometrically maximal abelian covering X′
2(p) →X0(p) of X0(p) over Q
unramiﬁed outside cusps is cyclic of degree 2Np.
