Previous KU: The study aimed to verify if finger representations in the primary sensorimotor cortex are spatially compressed in musicians with dystonia by analyzing passive right-hand movements. Researchers applied multiple spatial metrics, including peak activation, center of gravity, and softmax-weighted coordinates, to map finger locations on flattened cortical surfaces. Statistical comparisons of these spatial layouts between patient and control groups were conducted using MANOVA and t-tests on Euclidean distances.
Next KU: The study evaluated spatial and multivariate methods to characterize finger representations in the primary sensorimotor cortex, noting substantial overlap and individual variability in activation patterns. Spatial metrics like center of gravity and maximum activation showed low reliability, whereas a softmax-weighted approach with k=0.8 provided the best compromise. Multivariate pattern analysis using cross-validated Mahalanobis distances demonstrated significantly higher reliability than spatial measures, revealing a consistent dissimilarity structure where thumb and ring fingers were most distinct.
