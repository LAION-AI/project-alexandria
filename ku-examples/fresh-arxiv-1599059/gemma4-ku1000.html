Recorded context summary

The paper proposes an implementation of the adiabatic quantum search algorithm using a cavity-laser-atom system. It employs an arrangement of three atomic layers within a single-mode chamber, where the marked state is identified by an energy difference between the ground states. The process, known as inverse, fractional, stimulated Raman Adiabatic Transition (if-STIRAP), transforms the system from an initial entangled state into the single marked state within a time proportional to the square root of N.
