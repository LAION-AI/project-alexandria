Recorded context summary

A randomized controlled study evaluated the impact of a six-month fasting-mimicking diet on metabolic markers and albuminuria in patients with type 2 diabetes and diabetic nephropathy. The intervention group showed significant improvements in microalbuminuria and insulin resistance compared to a Mediterranean diet control group, although these benefits were not sustained at the three-month follow-up. The study suggests that the lack of change in DNA damage and dicarbonyl detoxification markers may contribute to the relapse of albuminuria.
