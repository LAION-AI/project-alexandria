Recorded context summary

A randomized controlled study compared a fasting-mimicking diet to a Mediterranean diet in patients with type 2 diabetes and diabetic nephropathy. The fasting-mimicking diet reduced microalbuminuria and improved metabolic markers like HOMA-IR and suPAR in the short term. The study suggests that the lack of changes in the detoxification of dicarbonyl compounds and markers for DNA damage may explain the recurrence of albuminuria at follow-up.
