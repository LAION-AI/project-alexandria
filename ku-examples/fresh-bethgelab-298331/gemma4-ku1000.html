Recorded context summary

A randomized controlled study assessed the effects of a six-month fasting-mimicking diet versus a Mediterranean diet on patients with type 2 diabetes and diabetic nephropathy. The study found that the fasting-mimicking diet reduced microalbuminuria and HOMA-IR, but not macroalbuminuria, with effects sustained only at the three-month follow-up. The researchers suggest that the lack of change in dicarbonyl detoxification and DNA-damage markers may explain the relapse of albuminuria.
