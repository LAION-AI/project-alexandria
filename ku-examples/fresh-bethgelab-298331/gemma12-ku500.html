Recorded context summary

A randomized controlled study evaluated the effects of a six-month fasting-mimicking diet versus a Mediterranean diet on patients with type 2 diabetes and diabetic nephropathy. The intervention significantly reduced microalbuminuria and improved metabolic markers like HOMA-IR and suPAR in the fasting-mimicking group. However, these improvements were not sustained at the three-month follow-up, potentially due to a lack of change in dicarbonyl detoxification and DNA-damage markers.
