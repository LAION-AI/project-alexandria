Recorded context summary

The authors propose a two-spring model with ultra-low effective tip mass to explain fine structure in atomic stick-slip motion observed in Friction Force Microscopy. This fine structure indicates that the tip apex can be partially or completely delocalized, challenging the traditional single-spring model. The model suggests that a rapid, thermally induced movement of the tip end can lead to a "stick-slip" behavior in the cantilever, while the contact between the tip and the surface remains decentralized.
