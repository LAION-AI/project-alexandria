Recorded context summary

The paper analyzes a two-mass-two-spring model to explain 'fine structure' in atomic stick-slip motion observed in Friction Force Microscopy. It demonstrates that an ultra-low effective tip mass leads to contact delocalization, where the tip apex moves rapidly between surface potential wells. This model provides a theoretical basis for recent experimental findings that were previously attributed to improbable multi-tip contact configurations.
